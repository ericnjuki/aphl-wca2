# Survey

The Survey page is where staff complete competency self-assessments.

## Starting a new assessment

1. Pick a **Domain** from the dropdown in the top-left (e.g. *Bioinformatics*, *Administrative Controls*).
2. The **Start** page shows the domain's **Purpose** and **Introduction** (when an admin has added them) so you know what the framework covers. Click **Start** to begin.
3. For each subcompetency, pick the level that best describes your current ability. Each option is prefixed with its level in **bold**, followed by what that level looks like in practice:
   - **Beginner** — needs guidance to perform the task
   - **Competent** — can perform the task unsupervised
   - **Proficient** — can teach others and solve edge cases
   - **Expert** — authoritative; the person others consult
   - **N/A** — the task is outside your role

## Footnotes

Some terms in the questions are marked with a symbol (e.g. `*`, `‡`). Their definitions appear in a **footnotes** block at the bottom of the page — only the symbols actually used on that page are listed. The generic `*` marker means the term is defined in **Appendix B** (the glossary).

## Saving and resuming

- The survey **auto-saves** your progress to your browser as you answer (debounced ~2 seconds). If you close the tab mid-assessment, your answers are kept.
- The **Save** icon in the top-right also saves your progress to the server.
- If there's an unfinished session for the domain you pick, you'll see an amber **Unfinished assessment** banner with **Start over** and **Resume** buttons. Resume picks up exactly where you left off.

## Submitting

Click **Submit** on the last page. After submission:

- The assessment enters `pending` review status (a reviewer has to approve it before it counts in reports).
- The **My assessments** page adds a completed card with your average score.
- You can start the same domain again to record a newer snapshot.

## Theme

The survey widget follows the app theme — toggle light/dark with the sun/moon icon, and the survey re-themes live without losing your answers.
